Programs

Empower through education

We partner with grassroot organisations to identify and financially support aspiring girls for higher education. We are a 100% pass-through organisation so your entire donation goes directly to fund the students

Inspire via community

We celebrate role models that inspire. Provide encouragement by sharing stories and guidance to overcome difficulties. Introduce young minds to new experiences that shape them into more confident individuals.

Pay it forward

We believe in paying it forward as it inspires generosity by helping everyone discover the joy of giving. The person paying it forward grows as much as the person receiving timely help. This often helps create role models and build a strong community.
