Partners

Turning Point Educational Trust

Turning Point Educational Trust a non-profit organization, was founded with the vision to promote innovative solutions to education challenges and inspire actions that create sustainable future for children. Slum children’s opportunities to learn are being threatened due to various reasons and we work to stimulate a learning environment that provides knowledge and skills. We operate in Chennai’s largest resettlement slum in Kannagi Nagar and Perumbakkam through Motivation and Learning Centers and direct community interventions.

Society for Education and Action

Society for Education and Action shortly known as SEA, is a Non Governmental Organization located in Mamallapuram in the Tamil Nadu region of India. SEA was founded in the year 2000 by a community of development workers and young people from 32 local fishing villages in and around the area. SEA has mainly been working for Children’s Rights, Education and Protection.

AID INDIA

AID (Association for India’s Development) and its sister organisation AID INDIA in Chennai work with grassroots communities across India on education, livelihoods, health, women’s empowerment and social justice. In Tamil Nadu, their Eureka programme runs schools, free evening tutoring and village libraries, and their You GO Girl! initiative gives girls bicycles so that distance doesn’t end their schooling. As one of Nia’s partners, AID INDIA helps us find girls who need support to continue their education.

I Believe I Can (IBIC)

At Nia, we believe that empowering young women goes beyond sponsoring education. It’s about providing the guidance and mentorship they need to succeed. This shared vision forms the foundation of our partnership with I Believe I Can (IBIC). IBIC is a vibrant community committed to supporting women at the grassroots level through empathetic mentoring, skill development, and career guidance. With programs ranging from one-on-one mentoring to workshops and networking circles, IBIC helps women unlock their potential and pursue their dreams. Together, we aim to inspire, uplift, and create a brighter future for young women everywhere.
