#### The first in a series of nia events exploring and celebrating the power of choice

This is the first in a series of events that we have been busy planning here at Nia. Our aim is to provide thought-provoking conversation between inspiring industry leaders focused on the driving forces behind their success. Providing attendees with transformative insight, a look at our work with nia and an opportunity for virtual networking.

These conversations will give us the opportunity to hear some personal stories of pivotal moments and help us to gain insight into how we may navigate our choices to make the best of our lives.

![Nia Pathways virtual event](assets/images/nia-pathways-social.webp)

**Speakers:** Shobana Radhakrishnan and Arun Mani

#### Meet the Speakers

Our first event is coming up in just two weeks and features the founder of Nia, Arun Mani, and one of our board members, Shobana Radhakrishnan, who is also Senior Director of Engineering at Google TV. Arun and Shobana have been friends since their college days in India, and will be using this time to discuss their own careers and how their personal achievements have translated into their involvement with Nia, dedicating their time to empowering girls through education.

#### Register now

Join us on at **10:00 AM PST / 1:00 PM EST, 27 July 2024** on LinkedIn Live for engaging conversation, personal anecdotes and practical advice about how to navigate your career and make the best choices for you. Be inspired by Nia’s success stories and enjoy opportunities to connect with like-minded advocates for social change and women’s empowerment.
